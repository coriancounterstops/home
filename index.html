What are Corian countertops?

Maintenance

Simple habits help protect your countertop

Follow manufacturer recommendations for your specific Corian product and use everyday habits that reduce unnecessary wear.

01

Clean Regularly

Wipe spills and use an appropriate household cleaner with a soft cloth or sponge.

02

Use a Cutting Board

Cut food on a dedicated board rather than directly on the countertop surface.

03

Protect From Heat

Use trivets or heat-resistant pads under hot cookware.

04

Professional Repairs

Have significant scratches, cracks or other damage assessed by a qualified professional.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Corian countertop questions

Are Corian countertops easy to clean?

Corian has a nonporous surface and routine cleaning is generally straightforward. Follow the manufacturer's recommended cleaning instructions.

Can Corian countertops be repaired?

Certain surface damage can be repaired or refinished depending on the type and severity of the damage.

Can Corian be custom fabricated?

Yes. Solid-surface fabrication can support customized shapes, edges, cutouts and other design details.

Can Corian be used in bathrooms?

Yes. Corian is used for bathroom countertops and vanity applications where a smooth, nonporous surface is desirable.
